The frontier AI lab behind Claude, preparing for an IPO.

- **Edition 001 (9 October 2026):** its IPO prospectus, seen by Reuters, showed [nearly $4.6 billion in 2025 revenue](https://www.reuters.com/business/finance/anthropics-ipo-prospectus-shows-sweeping-ai-vision-surging-costs-2026-09-28/) and an $8.06 billion operating loss. - **Edition 003 (23 October 2026):** is expected to [publicly file its IPO prospectus](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/article/anthropic-to-publicly-file-for-ipo-as-early-as-friday-sources-say-135343841.html) as soon as 23 October, aiming to list in November. [Read the edition](/keeping-up-with-the-capital/editions/2026-10-23-anthropic-ipo-openai-bridge-round/)
- **Edition 004 (30 October 2026):** made its prospectus public, showing a [$974 million operating profit](https://dealroom.co/news/info-1sghj5n-anthropics-s-1-takeaways-from-giant-compute-commitments-to-complex-lock/) in Q2 and $417 billion in computing commitments it can't cancel. [Read the edition](/keeping-up-with-the-capital/editions/2026-10-30-anthropic-s1-profit-compute-bills/)
- **Edition 005 (6 November 2026):** swung back to a [$567 million operating loss](https://fortune.com/2026/11/02/anthropics-growth-slowed-and-costs-jumped-in-the-third-quarter-updated-ipo-filing-shows/) in Q3, mainly because it rented $5 billion of short-term computing to keep up with demand. [Read the edition](/keeping-up-with-the-capital/editions/2026-11-06-anthropic-q3-loss-short-term-compute/)
- **Edition 006 (13 November 2026):** set its IPO range at [$70 to $78 a share](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/10/anthropic-sets-ipo-price-range-of-70-to-78-per-share-to-reach-an-implied-valuation-of-1-404-trillion/), up to $1.4 trillion, and drew [about six times the orders](https://www.siliconrepublic.com/business/bloomberg-anthropic-ipo-six-times-oversubscribed-ai) it needed.
